🏛️ COMMAND HUB

You start here. Walk near any terminal and press E to enter that zone. The ⚡ Power Grid (boss) is locked until you complete all 4 other zones.

🏥 Hospital 🚦 Traffic 🏧 ATM 🏠 Smart Home ⚡ Boss (Locked)

🏥 Zone 1 – Hospital Network

  • 🔴 Walk into red glowing virus nodes to destroy them
  • 🔌 Hold E near the top of each server line to reconnect it (4 total)
  • 💾 Once all servers are connected, find the BACKUP node on the right → press E
  • ✅ Zone complete when all viruses are gone + backup activated
  • ⚠️ Viruses spread over time — be fast!

🚦 Zone 2 – Traffic Grid

  • 🚗 Cars will crash if traffic signals are wrong — you have max 8 crashes
  • 🔄 Walk near a signal and press SPACE to rotate it
  • ✅ Signal turns green when it points the correct direction
  • 🛡️ Fixed signals slow nearby cars to prevent crashes
  • ⚠️ Fix all 8 signals to complete the zone

🏧 Zone 3 – ATM Banking Network

  • 🔴 Red diamond packets = malicious — touch them while pressing SPACE or F
  • 🟢 Green square packets = safe — ignore them
  • 🔐 Walk to the 4 purple lock icons and press E to activate encryption
  • 🛡️ Active encryption nodes auto-block nearby malicious packets
  • ⚠️ If too many malicious packets escape, the overload meter fills — zone fails!

🏠 Zone 4 – Smart Home Tower

  • 📱 Walk to red blinking devices and hold E to secure them (wait for bar to fill)
  • 📷 Avoid the sweeping red camera cones — they raise the alarm
  • 🚨 If the alarm meter maxes out, you get kicked back to the hub
  • 💡 Watch camera rotation patterns — sneak between sweeps
  • ✅ Secure all 8 infected devices to complete the zone

⚡ Zone 5 – Power Grid Boss Fight

  • ⚡ Collect yellow ⚡ energy fragments to fill your charge meter (5 fragments = full)
  • 🌊 Dodge expanding EMP rings — they drain your charge!
  • 🔫 When fully charged, press F or SPACE to fire a beam
  • 🛡️ The boss has rotating purple shields with gaps — shoot through the GAPS
  • 💥 Hit the boss 4 times to destroy the AI Core
